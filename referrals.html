🧭 Referral Guide

Sometimes the best help comes from a qualified person or organization. TraceGuide AI can help you identify what type of support may be appropriate, but it does not replace professional advice.

🆘 Immediate Safety

If you are in immediate danger or believe someone may be at risk of physical harm, contact local emergency services or a trusted person who can help you safely.

🔐 Cybersecurity & Account Problems

Consider a qualified cybersecurity professional when you are dealing with possible account compromise, suspicious logins, device security problems, malware concerns, or other technical issues.

📱 Harassment & Technology-Facilitated Abuse

A victim advocate or technology-safety specialist may be able to help with safety planning, documentation, privacy concerns, unwanted contact, and technology-related abuse.

⚖️ Legal Questions

If you need legal advice about harassment, threats, privacy, impersonation, or other matters, consider speaking with a qualified attorney or appropriate legal-aid organization.

TraceGuide AI does not provide legal advice or determine whether someone has committed a crime.

❤️ Emotional & Recovery Support

Difficult online experiences can be exhausting. A qualified counselor, mental-health professional, crisis service, trusted person, or victim-support organization may be able to provide additional support.

📋 Documentation Support

If you need help organizing what happened, keep original information when possible and record dates, times, messages, usernames, URLs, screenshots, and other observable events.

Avoid changing or deleting important evidence before receiving appropriate professional guidance when preservation may matter.

🛡️ A Note About AI

TraceGuide AI can help organize information and identify observable patterns in material that a person chooses to provide.

AI findings should not automatically be treated as proof of identity, intent, guilt, or responsibility. When important decisions are involved, qualified human professionals should review the situation.
