Understanding Common Concerns

📱

Account Compromise

Unexpected login alerts, password changes, unfamiliar devices, or unusual account activity may deserve investigation.

👤

Impersonation

Someone may create an account or profile that appears to represent another person. Preserve the observable information safely.

💬

Online Harassment

Repeated unwanted messages, threats, or abusive online behavior can be documented and may warrant additional support.

🔎

Personal Information Exposure

If personal information appears online unexpectedly, document where it appeared and consider appropriate privacy and safety steps.

AI Should Assist — Not Accuse

TraceGuide AI is designed to help organize information and identify observable patterns in material that a person chooses to provide.

AI findings should not be treated as proof of someone's identity, intent, guilt, or responsibility. Important decisions should involve appropriate human review and professional expertise.

Protect Your Privacy

Do not share passwords, authentication codes, banking information, private keys, or other information that could put you at risk.

Only document or share information when it is safe for you to do so. If you believe your device or account may be monitored, consider using a safer device or trusted person when seeking help.
