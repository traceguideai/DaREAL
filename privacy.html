Privacy

Privacy is a core principle of TRACEGUIDE AI. People seeking help with sensitive experiences should have control over their information.

Prototype notice: This website is currently a prototype. It is not yet a production confidential evidence-storage service. Do not submit passwords, financial information, authentication codes, or other highly sensitive information through this prototype.

Our Privacy Principles

  • Collect only information that is necessary for the service being provided.
  • Give users meaningful control over information they provide.
  • Explain how information is used before collecting it.
  • Protect sensitive information using appropriate security controls.
  • Provide appropriate deletion and retention controls as the service develops.
  • Never secretly monitor people or devices.
  • Never access someone's accounts without authorization.

Information You May Enter

The prototype may allow you to enter basic descriptions, dates, notes and other information needed to organize an experience.

However, you should avoid entering:

  • Passwords
  • Authentication codes
  • Private encryption keys
  • Bank or credit-card information
  • Government identification numbers
  • Highly sensitive personal information

Evidence & Confidentiality

A future TRACEGUIDE AI evidence system would require strong technical and organizational safeguards, including authentication, encryption, access controls, secure storage, audit logging, retention policies and deletion mechanisms.

Those protections must be properly implemented and independently reviewed before TRACEGUIDE AI should be used to store real sensitive case evidence.

AI & Your Information

AI-assisted analysis should be limited to the information necessary for the requested task. AI-generated observations should not automatically be treated as facts about another person.

TRACEGUIDE AI is designed around the principle: AI should assist — not accuse.

No Secret Surveillance

TRACEGUIDE AI does not promote covert monitoring, unauthorized access, retaliation or hacking.

Any technical investigation should be performed with appropriate authorization and, where necessary, by qualified cybersecurity or telecommunications professionals.

Future Privacy Commitments

As TRACEGUIDE AI develops, our privacy framework should address data collection, processing, storage, sharing, retention, deletion, security incidents and user rights in clear language.

The production service should also undergo appropriate legal, privacy and security review before handling sensitive user evidence.

Questions

If you have questions about the project or its privacy approach, visit our contact page.
