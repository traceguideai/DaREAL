Who Is TraceGuide For?

TraceGuide AI is designed for people who are experiencing concerning technology-related situations and need help understanding their options.

🛡️ Digital Safety

Help with account security, suspicious activity, privacy concerns, and safer digital practices.

📱 Online Harassment

Guidance for people experiencing repeated unwanted contact, harassment, impersonation, or cyberstalking concerns.

📋 Documentation

Help organize dates, messages, screenshots, URLs and other information into a clearer timeline.

🤝 Human Support

Help people identify appropriate cybersecurity, legal, advocacy, and recovery resources.

How You Can Help

📣 Share

Tell someone about TraceGuide when you believe the resources may be useful to them.

🏢 Community Organizations

Organizations can share TraceGuide as an additional educational and support resource.

💻 Cybersecurity Professionals

Qualified professionals may become part of a future referral network for people who need specialized assistance.

❤️ Advocates & Supporters

Help people feel heard and respected without judging or dismissing their experiences.

How to Refer Someone Safely

If you know someone who may benefit from TraceGuide, you can simply give them the website and allow them to decide whether they want to use it.

Never publicly share another person's private messages, screenshots, location, passwords, account information, or other sensitive material without their permission.

If someone is in immediate danger, encourage them to contact local emergency services or a trusted person first.

🔐 Protect People's Privacy

TraceGuide AI should never be used to expose, retaliate against, threaten, hack, or secretly monitor another person.

When sharing TraceGuide, encourage people to protect their personal information and avoid sending passwords, banking information, security codes, private keys, or other information that could put them at risk.

Our Approach

1. Listen

Give people a place to explain what they are experiencing.

2. Document

Organize observable information and preserve useful records.

3. Protect

Strengthen digital safety and reduce avoidable risks.

4. Connect

Help people find appropriate human and professional support.

AI Should Assist — Not Accuse

TraceGuide AI can help identify observable patterns in information provided by a user. AI findings are not independent proof of identity, intent, guilt, or responsibility.

Serious concerns should be reviewed with appropriate qualified professionals when necessary.
