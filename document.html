Good documentation habits

  • Record what you directly observed rather than assumptions about who caused it.
  • Keep the original date and time whenever possible.
  • Keep screenshots or original messages when it is safe to do so.
  • Record the platform, account name, URL or other source when relevant.
  • Never share passwords or authentication codes as evidence.
