Follow-Up Guide

Listen. Document. Protect. Connect. Recover.

Purpose

Follow-up helps ensure that a person who contacts TraceGuide AI understands their next available step and knows where to seek additional support when needed.

01 — Review the Initial Request

Confirm that the person's request has been reviewed and identify the primary support category.

02 — Check Safety Again

Consider whether the person's circumstances may have changed since the original request.

If there is an immediate concern about physical safety, encourage appropriate emergency assistance or contact with a trusted person.

03 — Provide the Next Step

Give the person a clear and practical next step whenever possible.

  • Digital security guidance
  • Documentation assistance
  • Cybersecurity professional
  • Advocacy or victim-support service
  • Legal information or qualified legal support
  • Emotional or recovery support
04 — Respect the Person's Choice

People should be allowed to make informed decisions about what they do next. TraceGuide AI should provide options rather than pressure someone into a particular action.

05 — Follow Up Safely

Use the person's preferred and reasonably safe method of communication when available.

Do not expose sensitive information through public posts, unsecured messages, or unnecessary communications.

06 — Encourage Continued Documentation

When it is safe to do so, encourage the person to maintain a simple chronological record of observable events and preserve relevant original information.

07 — Know When to Refer

TraceGuide AI should not attempt to handle situations outside its appropriate scope. When specialized assistance is needed, help the person identify an appropriate qualified professional or organization.

AI Safety Rule

AI should assist — not accuse.

Follow-up must never present an AI-generated interpretation as proof of identity, intent, guilt, responsibility, or the cause of an unexplained event.

Privacy Reminder

  • Never request passwords.
  • Never request authentication codes.
  • Never request banking credentials or private keys.
  • Share information only when necessary and appropriate.
  • Respect the person's privacy and control over their information.

Final Question

Have we helped this person identify a safer and more appropriate next step?
