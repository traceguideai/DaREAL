AI Safety Principle

AI should assist — not accuse.

TraceGuide may use AI to organize information and identify observable patterns in submitted material. AI should not independently establish someone's identity, intent, guilt, responsibility, or the cause of an unexplained event.

Human judgment and appropriate professional support remain essential.
Privacy Principle

Collect only information necessary for the support request. Never request passwords, authentication codes, banking information, or private keys.

The current prototype should not be treated as a secure evidence vault. Sensitive evidence handling will require a properly secured backend, access controls, encryption, retention policies, and appropriate privacy and security review.
