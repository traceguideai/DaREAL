Safety Guide

Protect Yourself. Protect Your Information.

If You Are in Immediate Danger

If you believe there is an immediate threat to your physical safety, contact local emergency services or a trusted person who can help you immediately.

Is Your Device Safe?

If you are concerned that someone may have access to your phone, computer, accounts, or communications, consider using a safer device before changing sensitive settings or contacting support.

Protect Your Accounts

  • Use strong, unique passwords.
  • Enable multi-factor authentication when available.
  • Review unfamiliar account sessions or devices.
  • Keep your phone and apps updated.
  • Never share passwords or authentication codes.

Document Safely

When it is safe to do so, record observable events with dates, times, sources, and relevant evidence.

Do not put yourself at greater risk simply to collect evidence.

Protect Your Privacy

  • Do not share passwords.
  • Do not share security codes.
  • Do not share banking credentials.
  • Do not share private keys.
  • Be careful when sharing your location or personal information.

TraceGuide AI Safety Principle

TraceGuide AI provides responsible guidance based on information submitted by the person seeking help.

AI may help identify observable patterns, but it does not independently establish identity, intent, guilt, or responsibility.
