About TRACEGUIDE AI

Helping people move from confusion to clarity through responsible technology, organized information and human support.

Our Mission

TRACEGUIDE AI is designed to help people experiencing concerning online activity or technology-facilitated abuse understand what they can observe, organize relevant information, strengthen their digital safety and find appropriate human support.

Our goal is not to create fear or make accusations. Our goal is to help people take practical, informed steps when something does not feel right.

What We Believe

Listen

People deserve to be heard respectfully, especially when they are confused, frightened or struggling to explain a technical experience.

Document

Clear records of observable events can help people understand their experience and communicate more effectively with professionals.

Protect

Digital safety should be practical, understandable and accessible to people regardless of their technical knowledge.

Connect

Technology should help people reach qualified human professionals when their situation requires expertise beyond an AI system.

Recover

Recovery is bigger than technology. People may also need trusted relationships, community resources, legal guidance and other forms of support.

What TRACEGUIDE AI Can Help With

  • Organizing timelines and information
  • Recognizing observable patterns in submitted material
  • Improving basic digital-security practices
  • Preparing information for discussion with qualified professionals
  • Finding appropriate human-support pathways
  • Learning about technology-facilitated abuse and digital safety

What We Do Not Do

  • We do not secretly monitor people or devices.
  • We do not hack accounts or computer systems.
  • We do not encourage retaliation or confrontation.
  • We do not identify alleged perpetrators based solely on AI analysis.
  • We do not treat an AI-generated possibility as established fact.
  • We do not present unverified claims as proven events.

AI Should Assist — Not Accuse.

AI can help organize information and highlight patterns, but technology can make mistakes and context matters. TRACEGUIDE AI should never be treated as the sole authority for determining identity, intent, guilt or responsibility.

When a situation involves serious technical, legal, medical or physical-safety concerns, qualified human professionals should be involved.

Our Vision

We envision a world where someone experiencing technology-facilitated abuse does not have to face the confusion alone.

A person should be able to say: “Something is wrong. I don't know what it is yet, but I know where to start.”

TRACEGUIDE AI is being built around that starting point.
