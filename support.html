Find Human Support

Technology can help organize information, but some situations need people with professional experience. You do not have to handle everything alone.

Immediate danger? If you are facing an immediate threat to your physical safety, contact your local emergency services or a trusted person who can help you immediately. TRACEGUIDE AI is not an emergency service.

🛡️ Digital Safety & Cybersecurity

A qualified cybersecurity professional may help review account security, device security and observable technical evidence.

  • Account security
  • Device security
  • Suspicious login activity
  • Digital evidence preservation

⚖️ Legal Support

A lawyer or legal-aid organization can explain your rights and possible legal options based on the facts of your situation and local law.

  • Harassment concerns
  • Threats
  • Impersonation
  • Privacy issues

💙 Victim & Survivor Support

Victim-service organizations and advocates can help with safety planning, emotional support, documentation and referrals.

  • Safety planning
  • Advocacy
  • Emotional support
  • Community resources

👥 Trusted People

Consider telling someone you trust what you are experiencing. Having another person aware of the situation can provide practical and emotional support.

  • Family
  • Friends
  • Community leaders
  • Other trusted people

What to bring to a professional

If it is safe to do so, organize the information you have before asking for assistance.

  • A chronological timeline of observable events
  • Relevant screenshots or original messages
  • Dates and approximate times
  • Usernames, account names or URLs when relevant
  • Descriptions of suspicious account or device activity

Important: evidence before conclusions

A concerning event can have multiple possible explanations. Keep records of what you directly observed and allow qualified professionals to investigate technical or legal questions.

TRACEGUIDE AI does not identify alleged perpetrators, determine guilt, or confirm an unverified explanation solely from information submitted by a user.
