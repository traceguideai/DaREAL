Case Review Framework

An internal guide for reviewing TraceGuide AI support requests consistently, safely, and responsibly.

Review Principle

A support request is a report from a person seeking help. It should be treated seriously and respectfully without assuming that every explanation or conclusion in the report has been independently established.

Listen → Verify what can be observed → Protect → Refer.

1 — Safety Review

Immediate Physical Safety

  • Does the person describe an immediate threat to life or physical safety?
  • Are threats of violence or imminent harm reported?
  • Does the person need emergency or urgent human assistance?

If immediate danger is reported, prioritize local emergency services, a trusted person, or an appropriate crisis/support resource.

Device and Account Safety

  • Does the person believe their device or account may be unsafe?
  • Could changing settings or contacting someone increase their risk?
  • Would a safer device or communication method be appropriate?

2 — Identify the Support Category

Primary Concern

  • Account security
  • Online harassment
  • Cyberstalking concern
  • Impersonation
  • Personal information exposure
  • Documentation assistance
  • Other technology-related safety concern

Secondary Needs

  • Cybersecurity professional
  • Victim advocate
  • Legal information or professional advice
  • Emotional or recovery support
  • Community resources

3 — Evidence Review

Separate Observation From Interpretation

Ask:

  • What happened that the person directly observed?
  • When did it happen?
  • Where or on which platform did it occur?
  • What documentation exists?
  • What information remains uncertain?

Useful Documentation

  • Original messages
  • Screenshots when safely obtained
  • Dates and times
  • URLs or platform information
  • Account notifications
  • Relevant device or security alerts

4 — Do Not Conclude

TraceGuide AI must not turn a person's report into an unsupported accusation.

  • Do not declare who is responsible without appropriate evidence.
  • Do not claim to know someone's intent without evidence.
  • Do not identify an alleged attacker based solely on AI analysis.
  • Do not present unusual technology explanations as established facts.
  • Do not encourage retaliation or confrontation.

AI-assisted observations should be described as observations or possible patterns that may warrant further investigation.

5 — Determine the Next Step

Low-Risk Information Support

Provide general guidance, documentation resources, and digital safety information when appropriate.

Professional Referral

Consider referral to an appropriate cybersecurity professional, advocate, legal professional, or other qualified support service.

Urgent Human Support

If the situation involves immediate physical danger or another urgent safety concern, prioritize appropriate emergency or qualified human assistance.

6 — Response Standard

Every response should aim to leave the person with a clear and safer next step.

A useful response should be:

  • Respectful
  • Calm
  • Non-judgmental
  • Evidence-aware
  • Privacy-conscious
  • Action-oriented

7 — Privacy Rules

  • Collect only information necessary for the support request.
  • Never request passwords.
  • Never request authentication codes.
  • Never request private keys.
  • Avoid unnecessary financial information.
  • Do not publicly expose private information.
  • Use secure systems for sensitive case information.

8 — Final Review Question

Before responding, ask:

“What is the safest, most useful next step we can provide without claiming more than the available information establishes?”
