Secure My Digital Life

Small security improvements can make a meaningful difference. Work through the checklist at your own pace.

Safety first: If you believe another person may have access to your device or accounts, consider using a safer device before making sensitive changes. If changing a password could alert someone who has access to an account, consider getting professional safety advice first.

Account Security

Use unique passwords Avoid reusing the same password across important accounts.
Enable multi-factor authentication Use MFA on email, banking, social media and other important accounts whenever available.
Review account recovery options Check recovery email addresses and phone numbers and remove ones you no longer control.
Review active sessions Look for unfamiliar devices or sessions in your account security settings.

Device Security

Keep your device updated Install operating-system and security updates from trusted sources.
Use a device passcode Protect your phone or computer with a strong passcode or password.
Review installed applications Remove applications you do not recognize or no longer need, when safe to do so.

Privacy & Information

Review privacy settings Check who can see your posts, profile information, location and contact details.
Protect sensitive information Never share passwords, authentication codes, private keys or full financial information with someone asking for them online.

Remember

  • Do not use the same password everywhere.
  • Never give anyone an authentication code sent to your phone or email.
  • Be cautious with unexpected links and attachments.
  • Use official websites or applications when changing security settings.
  • If something feels unsafe, pause and seek trusted human assistance.
