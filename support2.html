🤝 Find Human Support

You don't have to handle a difficult technology-related situation alone. TraceGuide AI can help you understand what type of support may be appropriate and help you organize what you already know.

🆘 If you are in immediate danger

TraceGuide AI is not an emergency service. If you believe you are in immediate physical danger, contact your local emergency services or a trusted person who can help you safely.

🧭 What kind of support do you need?

🔐 Account or Device Security

For suspected account compromise, suspicious logins, device security concerns, or other technical problems, a qualified cybersecurity professional may be appropriate.

📱 Online Harassment or Cyberstalking Concerns

A victim advocate or technology-safety specialist may be able to help with safety planning, documentation, privacy concerns, and available options.

⚖️ Legal Support

If you need legal advice, consider speaking with a qualified attorney or appropriate legal-aid organization. TraceGuide AI does not provide legal advice.

❤️ Emotional or Recovery Support

Technology-related abuse can be exhausting and overwhelming. A qualified counselor, mental-health professional, crisis service, trusted person, or victim-support organization may be able to provide additional support.

📋 Documentation & Evidence

You can organize information such as dates, messages, screenshots, usernames, URLs, and other observable events. Preserve original information when possible. Do not assume that an AI-generated pattern automatically establishes who is responsible.

🔎 How TraceGuide AI Approaches Reports

Listen. We take your concerns seriously without ridicule.

Document. We help organize information you already have.

Review. Observable patterns can be identified without automatically assigning blame.

Guide. We help identify what type of human support may be appropriate.

Refer. When a situation is outside our role, appropriate human or professional resources should be considered.

🛡️ Protect Your Information

Please do not submit passwords, banking information, security codes, private keys, or other information that could put you at risk.

Only share information that is necessary for someone to understand your request for support.
