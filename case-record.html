Case Record Framework

‹p class="intro"› Internal template for organizing a support request consistently, safely, and without turning the current public prototype into a sensitive case database.

IMPORTANT — TEMPLATE ONLY

This page is a framework for designing TraceGuide's future case-management process. Do not enter real names, private messages, passwords, financial information, authentication codes, private keys, medical information, precise addresses, or highly sensitive evidence into this public GitHub Pages prototype.

Real case records should eventually be handled through a properly secured private system with appropriate access controls, encryption, retention policies, audit procedures, and privacy protections.
Case Record Principle

Record what is necessary to understand the request and provide an appropriate next step. Separate observable information from assumptions, interpretations, and conclusions.
01 — CASE IDENTIFICATION

Basic Case Reference

Case ID Example: TG-0001
Date Received Record the date the request was received.
Current Status
New Under Review Referral Needed Follow-Up Closed
02 — REQUEST SUMMARY

What Is the Person Asking For?

Primary Concern Briefly summarize the request using the person's own information.
Requested Assistance Identify what the person wants help understanding or doing.
03 — SAFETY REVIEW

Immediate Safety

  • Physical-safety concern reported?
  • Device/account reported as unsafe?
  • Safer communication method needed?
  • Emergency or crisis resource potentially appropriate?
If there is an immediate threat to life or physical safety, appropriate emergency or crisis resources should take priority.
04 — SUPPORT CATEGORY

Identify the Main Need

  • Digital or account security
  • Online harassment
  • Cyberstalking concern
  • Impersonation
  • Personal information exposure
  • Documentation assistance
  • Legal-resource information
  • Emotional or recovery support
  • Multiple support needs
05 — OBSERVABLE INFORMATION

What Can Actually Be Documented?

Observed Events Record dates, times, messages, account activity, URLs, notifications, or other information that was actually observed or submitted.
Evidence Available Note the type of evidence without storing sensitive evidence here.
Unverified Interpretation Clearly distinguish assumptions or explanations that have not been independently established.
06 — DIGITAL SAFETY

Protection Considerations

  • Account-security guidance potentially needed
  • Multi-factor authentication guidance
  • Active-session review
  • Safer communication considerations
  • Documentation preservation when safe
  • Trusted-person involvement when appropriate
07 — REFERRAL DECISION

Choose the Appropriate Human Pathway

Referral Type
  • Emergency or crisis resource
  • Victim advocate
  • Cybersecurity professional
  • Legal resource
  • Specialized technology-abuse organization
  • Emotional/recovery support
  • Multiple referrals
Reason for Referral Explain why this type of human support appears appropriate based on the information available.
08 — RESPONSE

Prepare the Next-Step Response

  • Acknowledge the request.
  • Reflect the information actually provided.
  • Avoid unsupported accusations or conclusions.
  • Provide the appropriate next step.
  • Provide relevant referral information.
  • Respect the person's choices.
09 — FOLLOW-UP

Check the Outcome

  • Was the referral received?
  • Did the person's safety situation change?
  • Is additional support needed?
  • Is another referral appropriate?
  • Does the person want to continue?
10 — CLOSURE & LEARNING

Close Responsibly

When appropriate, document the final status of the request and identify lessons that could improve TraceGuide's resources or referral process.

Personal stories or information should never be reused for public education, research, or testimonials without appropriate consent and privacy safeguards.

REQUEST → SAFETY → CATEGORY → OBSERVABLE INFORMATION → PROTECT → REFERRAL → RESPONSE → FOLLOW-UP → CLOSURE
Final Case Question

“Based on the information we actually have, what is the safest and most appropriate next step for this person?”
